I am a fourth-year PhD student at the Sorbonne Economic Center (Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne University), working under the supervision of Lisa Chauvet and Jean Lacroix.

I graduated from Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne University with an MSc in Development Economics.

From April to July 2025, I visited the Institute of Economic History at Humboldt University in Berlin, sponsored by Prof. Dr. Nikolaus Wolf.

Research interests: economic history, political economy and development.
