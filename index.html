I am a fourth-year PhD student at the Sorbonne Economic Center, Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne University, working under the supervision of Lisa Chauvet and Jean Lacroix. I will defend my PhD dissertation in November 2026. My research interests are Development Economics, Political Economy and Economic History.

From April to July 2025, I visited the Institute of Economic History at Humboldt University in Berlin, sponsored by Prof. Dr. Nikolaus Wolf.

I graduated from Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne University with an MSc in Development Economics.
